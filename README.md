# eepy
There are always apps that count down to the next time you’ll see your partner again. 
But sometimes that date is too far away, and what you’re really looking forward to is the next call, the next small moment together.

For long-distance couples, sleep and time zones are some of the hardest things to manage. Someone is always staying up too late, waking up too early, or sacrificing rest just to squeeze in time. Over time, that gets exhausting, you start making time that works against each other instead of working together to be better.

And over time, that doesn’t just affect the relationship — it affects your health, your discipline, your routine. Instead of supporting each other’s growth, you can unintentionally enable burnout. What if waking up/ sleep became something you protected together?

I’ve always found that waking up to your partner’s voice or a photo they took just for you feels different. It feels like you both showed up for each other, even through the distance. Shared routines — especially around something as vulnerable as sleep — can bring couples closer in ways that big countdowns never do.

Eepy turns waking up into a shared promise. The photos and voice memos aren’t just sent randomly — they’re made specifically for that moment, meant to be opened only within your wake an sleep window. It creates a kind of shared accountability between you and your partner: you both wake up and sleep in the time you promised yourself, and in return, you get something that was created just for you. The exclusivity makes it sweet. The effort makes it meaningful. It’s about building better habits together. Supporting each other’s sleep. Encouraging discipline gently, not through pressure — but through presence.
